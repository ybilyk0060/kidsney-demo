Support and account deletion

Kidsney — fitness game for the whole family

Write to us at support@kidsney-sport.com. We answer within 72 hours, usually much faster.

How we handle data is described in the Privacy Policy (privacy.html).

Delete your account in the app

  • Open Parents → Settings → “Delete account”, then type DELETE. The account and the data of all children are deleted permanently, right away.
  • Only one child profile: on “Who's playing today?” tap “⚙️ Manage profiles”, pass the parent check and tap 🗑.
  • Deleting the account does not cancel a store subscription — cancel it first in “Manage subscription” (App Store or Google Play).

Ask us to delete your account by e-mail

Write to support@kidsney-sport.com from the e-mail address you sign in with (or tell us that address) with the subject “Delete my account”. We confirm the request and delete the account and all its data within 30 days, usually much sooner.

A guest account without an e-mail cannot be matched to an e-mail request — delete it in the app as described above.

What is deleted

  • The parent account: e-mail, sign-in, year of birth, language and reminder settings, the parent PIN (stored only as a hash) and the consent records.
  • All child profiles: nickname, avatar and age group.
  • All game progress: heroes, levels, rewards, quests, streaks and fight results.
  • All workout results and the usage events linked to the children's profiles.
  • Push tokens for reminders, the subscription status stored with us and the free-trial records.

What is not deleted

  • A store subscription — it keeps running until you cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.
  • Purchase records at Apple, Google and RevenueCat (RevenueCat knows only a random account ID, not your e-mail) — kept under those companies' own policies.
  • Anonymous sign-up and purchase statistics that are no longer linked to the account — up to 13 months.
