Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to support@kidsney-sport.com — we answer within 2 working days.

The camera does not see the child: put the phone 2–2.5 m away at chest height so the whole body is in view, and make the room brighter.

Squats are not counted: squat a bit deeper and stand facing the phone.

No room for the camera, or squats are hard for your child: “Parents” → check → turn on “No-camera mode”. The child then taps a big button to push.

Delete all data: “Parents” (below the arena) → check → “Delete all data”.
