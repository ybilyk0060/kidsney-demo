Support

About the game: Kidsney Zoo is a sumo game for children aged 4–8 in which the child moves for real. Every squat in front of the phone makes their animal push the opponent out of a friendly ring. A match lasts 40 seconds; for wins the child collects stars (up to 30 a day) and unlocks new animals. No ads, no chat. Without a subscription — 2 matches a day; the optional Kidsney Zoo Premium subscription (bought by a parent behind a grown-up check) removes the limit. Manage or cancel it in your App Store / Google Play account settings; “Restore purchases” is in the subscription screen.

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to support@kidsney-sport.com — we answer within 2 working days.

The camera does not see the child: put the phone 2–2.5 m away at chest height so the whole body is in view, and make the room brighter.

Squats are not counted: squat a bit deeper and stand facing the phone.

No room for the camera, or squats are hard for your child: “Parents” → check → turn on “No-camera mode”. The child then taps a big button to push.

New phone or reinstalled the app: if you saved progress with an e-mail, open “Parents” → check → “Saved progress before? Sign in”.

No voice: turn up the volume and check the sound button in the game; in “Parents” there is “Check sound”.

Delete all data: “Parents” (below the arena) → check → “Delete all data”.
