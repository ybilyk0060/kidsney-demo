Privacy Policy

17 November 2026

Kidsney Zoo is a game for children aged 4–8: the child squats in front of the phone, and their animal pushes the opponent out of a friendly sumo ring. A parent or legal guardian sets the game up; the child plays under adult supervision.

This policy explains in plain words what data the game uses, where it goes, why, and how to delete it.

1. The camera: video stays on this phone

The camera turns on only when the game starts or the camera button is pressed, and switches off when you leave the app; it is used to notice when the child squats. The picture is analysed right on this device by a pose-detection model that is built into the app — it is not downloaded from the internet.

Video, photos and sound are never recorded and never sent anywhere — not to us and not to anyone else. The body points the model finds are not saved or sent either: they exist only in the phone's memory while the match is running.

Only the match result leaves the device: at which moments of the match a push happened and how the match ended.

To get up-to-date exercises, the game downloads the public exercise list from our Kidsney catalog server; this request contains no data about the child or the family.

If other people are in the camera view, their image is processed on the device in the same way and is never stored or sent.

2. What we store on our server

Our server runs on Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). The game connects to it over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Database access rules let a family see only its own data.

  • Parent account: a guest account with a random ID and no e-mail. If you choose “Save progress”, an e-mail address and password (the password is stored only as a hash by Supabase). App language and time zone (to count the daily star limit).
  • Child profile: a nickname chosen by the parent (a real name is not needed), the year of birth (it sets how strong the opponent is) and the favourite animal. Up to 4 profiles per family.
  • Game progress: stars, opened animals and the history of rewards (how many stars and for what).
  • Matches: the match number (seed), the moments of each push, the result and the opponent's strength. Our server replays the match to confirm the stars.
  • Consent record: when you agreed, the version and language of the consent text and a fingerprint (hash) of that text.

3. Services that receive data

We do not sell personal data. The game shows no advertising, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, does not collect the device advertising ID and does not track the child across apps or websites.

  • Supabase (EU) — our server and database; it also sends the e-mail to confirm your address if you choose “Save progress”.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play — distribution of the game and payment for the optional Kidsney Zoo Premium subscription, under their own privacy policies. We never see card details.
  • RevenueCat (USA) — checks whether the subscription is active. It receives a random app user ID, the purchase receipt from the store and the device type; nothing about the child.

Voice tips are pre-recorded and built into the app; nothing is sent anywhere to play them.

4. What stays only on this phone

Video, sound and body points from the camera (section 1); the sound setting, the chosen animal and the signed-in session.

5. Consent and legal basis

Before stars are saved, the game asks the child to call an adult. The adult passes a check, confirms that they are the parent or legal guardian and agrees to the processing of the child's data; only then is a child profile created. Without consent the child can play without stars — then nothing about the child is stored on the server.

Legal basis: the parent's consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 8). Consent can be withdrawn at any time by deleting the data (section 7).

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, child profile, progress and opened animals — until you delete them.
  • Matches — 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • History of rewards — 12 months, then deleted automatically (the star balance stays in the progress).
  • Consent record — as long as the account exists.

7. How to delete data

In the game: “Parents” (below the arena) → pass the check → “Delete all data” → confirm. The account, the child profile, progress, matches and the consent record are deleted permanently.

You can also ask us by e-mail: support@kidsney-sport.com.

8. Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about your family, and have it corrected, exported or deleted; you can withdraw consent at any time. Write to support@kidsney-sport.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

9. Changes

When this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new date. If the change concerns the child's data, the game asks for consent again.
