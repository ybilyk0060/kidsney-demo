Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Kidsney is a fitness game for children aged 4–12. A parent or legal guardian sets the app up and manages it; the child plays under adult supervision.

This policy explains in plain words what data the app uses, where it goes, why, and how to delete it.

1. The camera: video stays on this phone

  • The camera is used only during a workout, to count your child's movements. The picture is analysed right on this device by a built-in pose-detection model.
  • Video, photos and sound are never recorded and never sent anywhere — not to us and not to anyone else.
  • The body points the model finds (positions of the joints) are not saved or sent either: they exist only in the phone's memory while the workout is running.
  • Only the workout result leaves the device: which exercise, how long it lasted, when each repetition was counted, a form score (0–100) and how clearly the body was visible (0–1).
  • If other people are in the camera view, their image is processed on the device in the same way and is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Without the camera the child can count moves by tapping “+1” (manual mode).

2. What we store on our server

Our server runs on Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). The app connects to it over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Database access rules let a parent see only their own family's data.

  • Parent account: e-mail and password, or Sign in with Apple / Google, or a guest account without an e-mail. When you register with an e-mail — your year of birth (to confirm you are an adult). App language, time zone, reminder time and the parent PIN (stored only as a hash).
  • Child profile: a nickname chosen by the parent (a real name is not needed), an avatar and the age group (4–5, 6–8, 9–12, 13–17 or 18+), stored as an approximate year of birth. Up to 4 profiles per family.
  • Game progress: heroes, levels, experience points, energy, quests, streaks, rewards and outfits; fight results with the sequence of in-game button presses (our server replays the fight to confirm the result) and the age group, which sets how easy the fight is.
  • Parents' marks: which of the four good deeds a parent marked for the child and on which day (no free text), and the hero's sleep time if a parent turned it on. They are deleted together with the child profile.
  • Workout results — as described in section 1.
  • Consent record: when you agreed, the version and language of the consent text and a fingerprint (hash) of that text.
  • Subscription status: product, store, transaction number and validity dates, received from RevenueCat. We never see card details.
  • Free-trial protection: a fingerprint (hash) of the e-mail address and a random installation ID, so that a free trial is given only once.
  • Usage events: which screens and steps were used (for example “workout started”, “offer shown”), with a random installation ID, the app version and the platform; workout and fight events may be linked to the selected child profile, while screen views and taps are recorded without it. No names, e-mail addresses, images or body points.
  • Error reports: the technical error text and the app version. E-mail addresses and identifiers are removed from the text on the device before sending; reports are sent only when signed in.
  • Performance measurements: how quickly the app starts, the frame rate in fights, how fast movement recognition works and the device performance class — numbers only, with the app version and platform, saved like usage events in our own database and not passed to anyone. They help us keep the game smooth on simpler phones.
  • Push token: only after the phone's notification permission is granted (the app asks an adult right after consent). Reminders to the parent are on by default once the permission is granted, at 18:00 unless the parent picks another time, and only between 07:00 and 19:00. A parent turns them off at any time in Parents → Settings → “Push notifications”, or in the phone's system settings.
  • Campaign labels: if the app was opened from our advertising link — the campaign labels (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content) and the domain of the referring site, saved once, without advertising identifiers.

3. Services that receive data

We do not sell personal data. The app shows no advertising, children never see ads, and the app contains no advertising SDKs; third-party analytics (PostHog) works only in the parents' area, as described above.

  • Apple App Store and Google Play — purchases and payments, under their own privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status. It receives a random account ID (not the e-mail and not the child's nickname) and purchase information.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — reports about app crashes, so we can fix errors. It receives the technical crash report (where in the app the error happened), the app version, the device model, the system version and a random Crashlytics installation ID. Crashlytics is switched off until a parent gives consent in the app: before that — including in the demo — no crash reports are sent to Google, and a report stored on the phone is sent only after consent. No account ID, e-mail, child's nickname, images or body points are sent. The device advertising ID is not collected on Android or iPhone (IDFA), Firebase Analytics is turned off, and the app does not ask for tracking permission.
  • PostHog (PostHog Inc., EU cloud) — usage statistics of the parents' area only. It starts working only after an adult passes the parent check; the child's screens send nothing to PostHog. It receives: which step in the parents' area happened (for example "plans screen opened"), the app version and platform, and a random installation ID kept on the device. It never receives the child's nickname, age or age group, game progress, camera images, the parent's name or e-mail, or the device advertising ID. No session recording and no automatic capture of taps.
  • Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — deliver reminders, only if a parent allowed notifications on the phone (reminders are then on by default and can be turned off in the Parent zone). A reminder may contain the child's nickname.
  • Sign in with Apple / Google — only if you choose that way to sign in.
  • Brevo (e-mail service) — sends account e-mails: address confirmation and password reset.

4. What stays only on this phone

  • Video, sound and body points from the camera (section 1).
  • Settings, language, the random installation ID, the signed-in session and the demo progress before registration.

5. Trying the app before registration

The demo works without an e-mail. Its progress is kept on the phone; only anonymous usage events with the random installation ID are sent; no crash reports are sent to Crashlytics until a parent gives consent. When you continue, the app creates a guest account without an e-mail (you can add an e-mail later in the Parent zone). A child profile is created only after a parent gives consent.

6. Consent and legal basis

A parent creates the child's profile after confirming that they are the parent or legal guardian and agree to the processing of the child's data. Legal basis: the parent's consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 8) and, for the subscription, the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)). Consent can be withdrawn at any time by deleting the child profile or the account (section 8).

7. How long we keep data

  • We keep data only as long as it is needed to run the game for your family: so that the child can continue the game and the parent can see progress.
  • Account, child profiles, progress and workout results — while the account is in use. If an account is not used, we delete it automatically together with all children's data: any account (with or without an e-mail) after 12 months without activity. Activity means opening the app and playing, training or signing in. Usage events linked to a child profile are deleted with that profile and in any case automatically 13 months after they were recorded.
  • 30 days before such a deletion we send a reminder to the parent's e-mail, if the account has one. Opening the app before that date keeps the account; a guest account is deleted without a reminder, because we have no way to contact you.
  • Usage events recorded without a child profile (linked only to the random installation ID, not to the account), anonymous sign-up and purchase statistics and error reports — up to 13 months; the reminder delivery log — 90 days.
  • Purchase records at Apple, Google and RevenueCat and crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics are kept under those companies' own policies.

8. How to delete data

  • A child profile: on “Who's playing today?” tap “⚙️ Manage profiles”, pass the parent check and tap 🗑. The profile and all of the child's progress, workouts, rewards and events are deleted permanently.
  • The whole account: Parents → Settings → “Delete account”, then type DELETE. The account and the data of all children are deleted permanently.
  • Deleting the account does not cancel a store subscription — cancel it first in “Manage subscription”.
  • You can also ask us by e-mail: support@kidsney-sport.com.

9. Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about your family, and have it corrected, exported or deleted; you can withdraw consent at any time. Write to support@kidsney-sport.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

10. Changes

When this policy changes, the new version appears here in the app with a new date.
