Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to the Kidsney mobile app and this website

Kidsney is a children's fitness game. It is designed for kids aged 6–12 and is operated in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR children's-privacy provisions (GDPR-K / Art. 8). This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, and how to delete it.

1. What we never do

  • Camera video never leaves the device. Workouts are analysed on-device with a pose-estimation model. We transmit and store only anonymous pose landmarks (coordinates of body joints) and derived exercise counters — never photos, never video, never audio recordings of your child.
  • We do not show behavioural advertising to children.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with data brokers.
  • We do not use third-party trackers or analytics SDKs inside the kids' experience.

2. What we store (parent account)

  • Parent email address and authentication credentials (managed by our authentication provider, Supabase, EU region).
  • Child profile: first name or nickname, year of birth, and in-game progress (levels, regions cleared, heroes unlocked, exercise statistics).
  • Optional weekly email report setting (off by default).

3. Legal basis and consent

The parent creates the account and gives verifiable consent for the child's data during setup (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), Art. 8; COPPA). Consent can be withdrawn at any time by deleting the account or the child profile — see below.

4. Subscription payments

Purchases are processed by the app stores (Apple App Store / Google Play) and, for subscription status, by RevenueCat. We receive only a purchase token and entitlement status — never card numbers.

5. Data retention and security

Pose landmarks are processed in memory on the device and discarded after each session; only session summaries (exercise counts, form score) are stored. Server data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is restricted by row-level security policies so a parent can only ever read their own children's data.

6. Your rights (delete everything)

Parents can, at any time:

  • delete a child profile (progress and stats for that child are removed);
  • delete the whole parent account together with all children and all stored data — in the app: Parent zone → Settings → Delete account;
  • request export or deletion by email: support@kidsney.app. We answer within 72 hours and complete deletion within 30 days.

7. Changes

If we materially change this policy, we will notify parents by email before the change takes effect.

8. Contact

Questions or complaints: support@kidsney.app. You may also contact your local data-protection authority; we cooperate with them fully.
